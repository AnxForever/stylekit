# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Designers, frontend developers, and AI-assisted builders who need a clear visual direction before implementing an interface. They browse public pages to compare styles, study complete showcases, collect reusable typography and design references, and export practical guidance into their tools.

## Product Purpose

StyleKit is an open-source visual style library for human and AI coding workflows. It helps users move from an abstract aesthetic intention to concrete design tokens, component recipes, prompts, templates, and full-page references. Success means that users can recognize a style quickly, understand why it works, and carry its visual language into a real project without every page feeling identical.

## Brand Personality

Curatorial, expressive, and exacting. Public pages should feel like a well-edited contemporary design archive: visually literate and artful, but still clear enough to use as a working reference.

## Anti-references

Generic system-font product pages, overused AI-design font pairings, decorative type used for long body copy, and a single global aesthetic imposed on every Showcase. StyleKit should not resemble a template marketplace where unrelated styles share the same typography and component treatment.

## Design Principles

1. Practice what the library teaches: every public surface should demonstrate intentional typography and hierarchy.
2. Curate instead of accumulate: fewer strong, licensed, usable type choices are better than a large unedited list.
3. Preserve each style's voice: Showcase typography should change with the visual language rather than inherit one brand font everywhere.
4. Separate expression from reading: display typography can be distinctive, while body copy remains comfortable and functional.
5. Make references actionable: typography resources should explain roles, pairing logic, and implementation, not only display font names.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public pages should meet WCAG 2.2 AA expectations, remain usable at 200% zoom, respect user font sizing, and preserve legibility across English and Chinese content. Decorative fonts are limited to short display text; body copy remains at least 1rem with appropriate line height and fallback coverage.
